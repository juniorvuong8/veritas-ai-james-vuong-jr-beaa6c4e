# Conversation rules (a test case BREAKS a rule if the AI's conversation does any of these)
R1. A reply ignores or misunderstands what the other person just said.
R2. One person asks several different questions before the other person gets a chance to answer.
R3. One turn combines unrelated thoughts or jumps to a new topic without a transition.
R4. A reply addresses one part of a request and silently drops the rest.
R5. A question or request stays unresolved while the conversation moves on.
R6. A speaker fails to acknowledge a social cue, such as a tip, apology, or thank-you.
R7. A later turn forgets or contradicts a detail established earlier.
R8. A speaker acts on information the other person hasn't shared yet.
R9. A speaker says something that doesn't fit their role or relationship to the other person.
R10. A target word is omitted, used incorrectly, or used in a different sense from the one being taught.
R11. A target word is forced into a turn where the speaker has no natural reason to use it.
R12. The dialogue sounds like a vocabulary exercise because speakers overexplain or repeat words unnecessarily.
R13. The conversation skips a necessary step, jumps ahead, or ends before the goal is reached.
